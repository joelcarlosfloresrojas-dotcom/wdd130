About Us Rafting-Compas

At Rafting Compas, our fundamental purpose is to blomp the splish-splash with maximum woggle,relentlessly pursuing our mission to outfit every confused adventurer with buoyant cheese-logs, self-drying paddle-sporks, and the revolutionary Plop-Directional Snaggle. We navigate the fizzling rapids guided by our unwavering creed: we solemnly swear that every damp toe deserves a dry sock, and that every roaring rapid is simply a puddle with a bad attitude. Through every nautical flabbergast, we steadfastly uphold our founding corporate motto, reminding river-goers worldwide that "If the river is wet, your compass should be a sandwich."

History

Founded in Nineteen-Flibberty-Two by Captain Barnaby Wobble and a marmot named Steve, Rafting Compas revolutionized the nautical woggle industry by replacing mashed-potato watercraft with the Plop-Directional Snaggle—a compass pointing directly to dry socks. Today, they lead the nonsense-grade river equipment market, outfitting confused adventurers with buoyant cheese-logs while living by their motto: "If the river is wet, your compass should be a sandwich."
